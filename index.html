Case Study 02  ·  BCDA Automation  ·  Illustrative

Eliminating Manual CMS File Processing at a 3,200-Beneficiary MSSP ACO

Two staff members spending 12–16 hours monthly downloading, validating, and reconciling CCLF files in Excel. Frequent errors in roster alignment causing downstream quality reporting failures.
Deployed bcda-client Python library with automated BCDA production pull, CCLF0–8 parser, three-way reconciliation engine, and Airflow DAG for weekly automation. Clean dbt gold-layer tables delivered to analytics team.
Python  ·  BCDA API  ·  dbt  ·  Airflow  ·  Azure
Outcome: CCLF processing time reduced from 3 days to under 4 hours. Roster accuracy improved from 91% to 97.4%.

Active Research Program  ·  Diagnostics AI for Resource-Limited Settings

LabFlow + Patient Portal + Clinical AI — A Full Diagnostic Stack for Sub-Saharan Africa

LabFlow, our deployed LIMS, provides the lab-side data infrastructure. The LabFlow Patient Portal closes the loop — delivering results directly to patients’ phones in plain language, in English or French, with no app required. Together they form the basis for clinical AI tools that require to function in resource-limited settings. Integris is also developing interpretable AI tools for diagnostic support in those same environments — where hematopathology expertise is scarce and enterprise diagnostic platforms are inaccessible. Our published research in bone marrow cytomorphology classification (EfficientNet-B3, 87.6% Top-1 accuracy, 21 morphologic classes, with Grad-CAM and SHAP explainability) provides the scientific foundation for a clinical decision-support tool currently under NIH SBIR development. Approximately 700,000 bone marrow examinations are performed annually in the US alone — the vast majority at large academic centers. In West Africa, hematopathology expertise is even more concentrated and inaccessible. Our research agenda explicitly includes validating interpretable diagnostic AI in low-resource settings, including district hospitals and reference laboratories in West Africa, as part of our global health and commercialization strategy.
